Small app, the right details

Names you can find again

Like “2026-11-03 Acme Energy – Invoice Electricity October”. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the on-device model adds the short subject. Choose your own pattern: the parts, their order, the separators and the date format.

English and German

An invoice is named “Invoice”, a Rechnung “Rechnung”. Or always use the language you pick.

Remembers where things go

The folder you used last time for that kind of document gets a Match badge, so the right tap is obvious.

Any folder in Files

iCloud Drive, On My iPhone and other locations that appear in Files. Create new folders in the picker.

Searchable PDFs

Every page gets an invisible text layer, so you can search, select and copy the text.

Open, share, rename, undo

Right after saving: look at the PDF, send it on, show it in Files, fix the name, or take it back.

Scan from anywhere

Open the scanner in one step from Control Center, the Lock Screen, the Action button, Back Tap or Siri (“Scan with Plopfile”). Or let Plopfile open straight to the camera when you start it.

Stays on your iPhone

Your scans are read and named on your iPhone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the on-device model helps; on others, Plopfile's built-in rules do the work. There's no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics, and Plopfile itself sends nothing anywhere. Privacy policy
