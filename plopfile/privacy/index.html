Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Applies to Plopfile for iPhone

In short: Plopfile does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own. Your scans are processed on your iPhone and saved only to the folder you choose.

Who we are

Plopfile is made by CB Group LLC (“we”). Questions about this policy: hello@cbgroup.global.

What Plopfile does with your documents

  • Camera. Plopfile asks for camera access only to run the iOS document scanner. Nothing is recorded outside a scan.
  • Text recognition and naming. Plopfile recognizes the text on every page with Apple's on-device text recognition, to make the PDF searchable, and uses the text of the first two pages to suggest a file name. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, it also asks Apple's on-device language model (the Foundation Models framework), which runs on your iPhone. Plopfile does not use Private Cloud Compute or any other server.
  • The unfinished scan. While you choose a name and a folder, the page images and the PDF are kept in Plopfile's own storage on your iPhone, so nothing is lost if you leave the app. This storage is excluded from iCloud and computer backups. It is deleted when you discard the scan, with your next scan, or when Plopfile starts again after the scan was saved. When you use Open or Share, a temporary copy of the PDF is made for the viewer or the share sheet; it is replaced by the next one and removed when Plopfile starts again.
  • The saved PDF. The PDF is written only to the folder you tap. If that folder is in iCloud Drive or belongs to another storage provider that appears in the Files app, that service stores and syncs the file under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Folders and settings. Plopfile remembers your favorite and recent folders (as references the system issues for them), which folder you used for each kind of document, and your settings. This stays on your iPhone, and iOS may include it in your iCloud or computer backups.

What we receive

Nothing from the app itself. We have no servers for Plopfile and no way to see your documents, file names or folders.

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and aggregated usage statistics for Plopfile. Apple provides these without identifying you; we use them only to fix problems. Purchases are handled by Apple; we receive sales reports from Apple that don't identify you.

Siri, Shortcuts and controls

If you start a scan with Siri, a shortcut, Back Tap, Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button, iOS only tells Plopfile to open the scanner. Apple handles Siri requests under Apple's privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and we delete the conversation when it's no longer needed. You can ask us at any time what we have kept from your emails, or to delete it.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes your IP address to deliver them (see GitHub's privacy statement). The pages use no cookies, analytics, external fonts or trackers.

Children

Plopfile collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can turn camera access off in iOS Settings › Plopfile at any time (scanning then won't work). Deleting Plopfile removes its storage, including folder references and settings. PDFs you saved stay in your folders until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date above. If a future version of Plopfile ever needed to collect data, we would say so here and in the App Store listing before it did.
