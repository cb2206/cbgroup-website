Getting started

How does Plopfile work?

Scan a document, check the suggested name, and tap the folder it belongs in. The PDF is saved there right away. Afterwards the Saved banner on the home screen lets you open, share, show in Files, rename or undo.

How do I add my folders?

On first launch Plopfile asks for the folders you save to most. Later, add, reorder or remove favorites in Settings (the gear on the home screen) under Favorite folders. You can also save to any other folder with “Other folder…” after a scan; it then shows up under Recent. New folders can be created inside the folder picker.

Which places can I save to?

Any folder you can pick in the Files app: iCloud Drive, On My iPhone, and other locations that appear in Files, such as the apps of other storage services. Some services occasionally end Plopfile's access; the folder is then marked “Reconnect”, and tapping it opens the folder picker so you can pick it again.

Plopfile opens the camera every time I open it. Can I turn that off?

Yes: Settings › Scanning › Open scanner on start. Control Center, the Action button, Siri and shortcuts still open the scanner directly.

File names

Where does the suggested name come from?

Plopfile recognizes the text on the first two pages and looks for the date, the sender and the type of document. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple's on-device model reads the document and also adds a short subject. On other iPhones, Plopfile's built-in rules look for the date, the sender (best when the letterhead names a company form such as Inc., LLC or GmbH) and common document types. Everything happens on your iPhone.

The name isn't ready yet, or it's wrong.

Until the suggestion arrives, the name field shows today's date and “Scan”, and you can already tap a folder. If the suggestion comes after you saved, Rename offers it. You can always type your own name before tapping a folder, or rename afterwards. Clear, flat, well-lit scans give the best results.

Can I change how names look?

Yes: Settings › File names › Pattern. Pick a preset or choose the parts (date, sender, type, subject), their order and the separator before each, the date format, and whether names follow the document's language or are always German or English. Parts that aren't found on a document are left out.

Which languages does Plopfile understand?

English and German documents. For other languages, or when there is little text, names use your iPhone's language if it is German, otherwise English. The app itself is in English.

What does “Match” next to a folder mean?

It's the folder you last used for the same kind of document, for example invoices. Nothing moves around; the badge just shows the likely choice.

Scanning from anywhere

The full steps are in the app under Settings › Scan from Anywhere. In short:

Control Center

Open Control Center, tap + at the top, then Add a Control. Search for Plopfile and choose Scan Document.

Lock Screen

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize › Lock Screen, remove one of the two buttons at the bottom and add Plopfile's Scan Document in its place. Your iPhone asks for Face ID or your passcode before the scanner opens.

Action button

On iPhones with an Action button: Settings › Action Button, swipe to Controls, tap Choose a Control, then search for Plopfile and choose Scan Document.

Back Tap

Create a shortcut with Plopfile's Scan Document action in the Shortcuts app. Then open Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap, choose Double Tap or Triple Tap, and pick your shortcut.

Siri

Say “Scan with Plopfile”. To say just “Scan document”, create a shortcut with Plopfile's Scan Document action and name it “Scan document”.

Your documents

Are my PDFs searchable?

Yes. Every page gets an invisible layer with the recognized text, so you can search, select and copy it in PDF viewers.

What does Undo do?

Undo deletes the PDF that was just saved and takes you back to choosing a name and folder, with the same scan. It's available until your next scan, or until Plopfile is closed.

I left the app before saving. Is my scan gone?

No. The home screen shows “Finish your last scan?” with Finish and Discard.

Does Plopfile upload my documents?

No. Plopfile has no servers and sends nothing anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Unfolded, the scanned pages appear large on one side while you name and file on the other.

Purchases

No. Plopfile is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.
